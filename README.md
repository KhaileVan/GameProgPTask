## 🐾 Pusa na si Paola
Retro 2D action-platformer built in Unity. Take control of Paola, recruit a helper companion, fight off pests, and find your way back home!

## 💡 Game Concept
You play as Paola, a cat who must return home, but the journey is filled with obstacles:

1. Pests and Combat
- The neighborhood is filled with aggressive animals like birds, rats, and dogs. Swing your paws to hit them!
- Every hit triggers a hitstop freeze and screen shake for satisfying impact feedback.
- Enemies flash bright red when damaged and fly off-screen on death.

3. Recruitable Companion (Khaile)
- Along the way, you can rescue Khaile (the companion cat). Once recruited:
- Khaile isn't scared of falling btw and will perform rescue double-jumps or escape hops to steer clear of water.
- Khaile automatically scans for nearby enemies and attacks them with a crescent slash to protect you.

3. Obstacles & Hazards
- Scared of Water!: Cats hate water. Landing in water or falling off screens is extremely dangerous.
- Falling Platforms: Some platforms shake for half a second when stepped on. Be fast!
- Cat Food Pickup: Collect that delicious cat food to progress to the next level!
- The House: Your ultimate goal. Lead both Paola and Khaile safely to their house to win the game!
